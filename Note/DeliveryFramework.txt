Delivery Framework
  a framework to gives you a plan for how to use the time in system design, making sure you get the working system while leaving enough room to demonstrate our technical depth
  Typical
    Clarify Requirement: 5 minutes
    Core Entities: ~2min
    Data flow (Optional)
    High-Level Design: 10~15 min
    Deep Dives: ~10min
  Remember this round is about communication. Thinking out loud
  Clarify Requirement
    Functional Requirement
      On this step you should keep this list as short as possible
      Typically: 3 requirement should be good enough
      And then mention other function is out of scope and ask interviewer is ok with that.

    Non-function requirement
      Thinking if the system should be highly available or the system should scale
      Make each requirement specific to the system that we're designing and where we can, put number in it
      For example:
          Dont mention low latency but to give number like low latency: <100ms.
      Make it clear the requirement thing such as Availability > consistency ? or consistency > availability. Since this is to follow CAP theorem
      Typically, all system will contains these checklist:
        Consistency vs Availability
        Environment constraints
        Scalability, read/write ratio
        Latency
        Fault Tolerance
        Compliance
        Our job is to pull out 3 to 5 that are uniquely interesting for this particular problem
          For example: In twitter case
            it's read-heavy system and more people read tweets than they do write them
            A feed can appear a few seconds late is fine
        Back of envelop
          This step we gonna calculate the traffic such as request per second based on the daily active user given by interviewer
          However, this step should be included if it's really worth to do
          So remember, you can talk to interviewer that you can do the math later when needed
          For example: later on, when you want to cache, you need to calculate the size that you gonna cache to see if it's worth to cache or have better way to cache given your key
  Core Entities
    It's the entities in your system and Persona is one of them
    If you're not clear, just look back at your function requirement to see what kind of entities will exist in your system design
    Try to make it simple as first and tell your interviewer it's just a first draft and we might end up coming back to it
    Recommendation: Do not mention the column/field yet. Leave it until the high level design so you will know which columns/field you need and you can go back later.
  API
    Most of the time, it is REST API
    And usually, 1 endpoint for 1 functional requirement
  Data flow
    This step is an optional way. However, if we design a system where it's an background thing and no customer facing.
    It is worth to have a short diagram about the data flow
  High level design
    The goal is to just get the simplest design down that satisfies your functional requirement first
    Tell your interviewer upfront that you gonna get something done E2E first and then address its limitation via mydeep dive later
      The easy way to do this is to endpoint by endpoint
      When you reach the database container, you can start to mention the field of data model that your design depends on
    As you go, you gonna see the problem with your design and that's a good thing. Just point them out and make a quick note and then keep going so you dont get stuck yet until you have that working high-level design
  Dive-deep
    In this step we gonna go back to our non-functional requirement and going 1 by 1 through each of them
      What needs to be faster?
      WHat need to scale?
    Senior level should be able to find those weak spots themselves and proactively drive the conversation
    Leading never means talking non-stop. You should give room for the interviewer to talk
    Remember the interview is about communication between 2 people and your teammate is trying ask help from you
    For example: "I'm gonna move on from this. Is there anything you want me to focus instead?"


  Q&A
    Q:What if the interviewer want to deep-dive questions before the high level design is done?
    A: This is fine. Likely they've seen enough of the basics and they want to get a specific signal
        Answer their specific question or if there's a gap, you can mention them out loud that you can come back later to answer them
